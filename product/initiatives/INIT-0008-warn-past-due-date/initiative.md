# INIT-0008 — A task added with a due date in the past gets a warning

## Outcome

_Stated at PRODUCT_DEFINITION._ As understood at INTAKE: when a person adds a
task with `ttc add` and gives a due date that is already in the past, `ttc`
tells them, so a mistyped date does not go unnoticed. Kept simple. Source: the
request (`product/sources/2026-11-24-requester-request.md`).

## Why now

The requester asked for it on 2026-11-24
(`product/sources/2026-11-24-requester-request.md`): "it's usually a typo and
I don't notice."
