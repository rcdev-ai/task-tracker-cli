# INIT-0007 — A task's due date is changed or removed in one short command

## Outcome

_Stated at PRODUCT_DEFINITION._ As understood at INTAKE: after a task was
added, a person using `ttc` can change its due date to a date they choose, or
remove it, with one short command each. Source: the request
(`product/sources/2026-10-31-requester-request.md`).

## Why now

The requester asked for it on 2026-10-31
(`product/sources/2026-10-31-requester-request.md`): "Today the only ways to
touch a due date after `ttc add` are `ttc postpone` and `ttc copy --due`."
